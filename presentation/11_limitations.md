# Limitations

While powerful, LungSenseAI is a research prototype with explicit constraints:

1. **Not Clinically Validated**: It does not replace a doctor.
2. **Dataset Bias**: The ICBHI dataset is relatively small and heavily imbalanced.
3. **Non-COPD != Healthy**: A negative COPD prediction does not rule out asthma, pneumonia, or other illnesses.
4. **Hardware Variance**: Real-world smartphone microphone quality varies wildly, affecting inference confidence.
