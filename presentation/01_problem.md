# The Problem

Respiratory illnesses like COPD and Asthma affect hundreds of millions globally. 
However, identifying specific acoustic abnormalities (crackles, wheezes) relies heavily on manual auscultation via a stethoscope, which is:
- **Subjective**: Dependent on the clinician's hearing and experience.
- **Inconsistent**: Prone to inter-observer variability.
- **Manual**: Lacks scalable, objective digital tracking.

There is a critical need for an objective, AI-assisted method to analyze respiratory audio and track longitudinal wellness data.
