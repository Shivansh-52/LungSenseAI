# Future Work

To evolve from a prototype to a clinical tool, the following steps are required:

- **Massive Multi-Center Datasets**: Collecting tens of thousands of varied audio samples.
- **External Clinical Validation**: Running double-blind studies against expert pulmonologists.
- **Hardware Normalization**: Pre-processing audio to normalize specific smartphone microphone acoustic footprints.
- **On-Device Inference**: Porting the PyTorch models to TFLite for completely offline, privacy-preserving edge inference.
