# The Dataset

The models were trained using the publicly available **ICBHI Respiratory Sound Database**.

- **Scope**: 920 WAV recordings from 126 patients.
- **Scale**: 6,898 individual annotated respiratory cycles.
- **Classes**: Normal, Crackles, Wheezes, and Both.
- **Disease Metadata**: Validated subsets of patients diagnosed with conditions ranging from Healthy to Asthma to COPD.

*Note: Dataset usage complies strictly with ICBHI citation constraints.*
