# Final Research Limitations

LungSenseAI is an educational and research prototype. The following limitations must be strictly acknowledged:

1. **Dataset Size**: The training dataset size is limited and may not capture the total variance of global respiratory patterns.
2. **Imbalanced Classes**: Disease classes are highly imbalanced, inherently biasing models toward majority classes.
3. **Rare Diseases**: Rare diseases within the dataset have insufficient patient support for reliable standalone classification.
4. **Research Prototype**: The COPD model is an experimental research prototype.
5. **No Clinical Validation**: External clinical validation has not been performed on live human subjects.
6. **Not a Diagnosis**: The system does not replace professional medical diagnosis.
7. **Non-COPD ≠ Healthy**: A negative COPD-pattern prediction does not mean the user is healthy. Other respiratory conditions may exist.
8. **Overlapping Sounds**: Respiratory sounds can overlap identically across fundamentally different underlying conditions.
9. **Hardware Variance**: Recording quality across different Android microphones can significantly affect prediction accuracy.
10. **Treatment Disclaimer**: Results should absolutely not be used to make treatment decisions.
